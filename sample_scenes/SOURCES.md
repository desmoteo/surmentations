# Sample scenes

Test scenes fetched from Wikimedia Commons, as served at up to 1920 px wide, chosen
to be unlike the reference photograph and unlike each other. They are inputs for
[`docs/generate_scene_examples.py`](../docs/generate_scene_examples.py), which renders
every effect over each of them into
[`docs/examples/scenes/`](../docs/examples/scenes/README.md).

They are tracked in the repository but are **not part of the package and not under its
MIT licence**: each keeps the licence listed below, and so does every image derived from
it. Reuse of the CC BY and CC BY-SA ones needs the author credited, and the BY-SA ones
shared under the same terms.
